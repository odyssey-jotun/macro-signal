# Macro Signal Messaging Canvas

- Campaign: Macro Signal launch
- Start date: November 2026
- Lead generator: [The Free Economic Report Guide](https://odyssey-jotun.github.io/macro-signal/guide.html)
- Commitment step: the free paper-trading demo

The canvas follows Donald Miller's StoryBrand Messaging Canvas: four rules, five foundational soundbites, three cornerstone tools, then a campaign map. Every asset below repeats the same words.

## The four rules

Every line on this page has to pass all four.

1. **Zero cognitive load.** A reader understands it on the first pass, with no finance vocabulary.
2. **Linked to survival.** It connects to money: protecting savings, growing wealth, saving time.
3. **Memorable and repeatable.** Someone can repeat it back after hearing it once.
4. **The customer is the hero.** The investor wins. The AI and I are the guide.

## The five soundbites

## The three cornerstone tools

### Tagline

**The economy, read for you.**

It names the category (the economy) and the offer (someone reads it for you), and it repeats back in five words. The elevator pitch carries the money link.

Candidates tested against it:

- Know what the news means for your money.
- Economic news in plain English.

### Elevator pitch

Lots of people see headlines about inflation and interest rates and have no idea what they mean for their money. I'm building an AI that reads the four economic reports that move the market the day they come out and explains each one in plain English. You understand the economic news in a few minutes and decide what it means for your own money.

### Brand story

Lots of people know the economy affects their savings, but the biggest reports come out at 8:30 on weekday mornings, buried in government tables. I used to miss every one of them, because they came out during class. So I'm building an AI that reads the four reports that move the economy the day they come out and explains each one in plain English. You'll know what inflation, jobs and interest rates are doing without hours of research. And the next time a headline hits, you'll know what it means for your money.

## What the campaign needs next

1. An email tool (Kit and Mailchimp both have free plans) so the guide page can trade the PDF for an email address. The sales and nurture emails start from that signup.
2. Version one of the agent, so the demo and the first weekly update have real reads to show.

## Guardrails

Macro Signal is a student project. Every asset says the AI trades a paper account with no real money, and none of them tell a reader to buy or sell anything. The short disclaimer goes at the bottom of every email and post:

> Student project. Paper trading only, with no real money. Nothing here is investment advice.
